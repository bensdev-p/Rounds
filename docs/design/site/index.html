Voice

Do

  • Use the word the way the wards do: an attending asking hard questions.
  • Stay on the student's side. Tease attendings, never students.
  • Let the personas carry the edge. The app itself stays plain and kind.
  • Keep every medical fact serious and sourced to a reviewed script.

Never

  • Anything from the word's street meaning: no hats, canes, chains, cash or sexual jokes.
  • Humiliation or hazing: no "you got owned", no leaderboards of shame.
  • Guilt about missed days, confetti or mascots.
  • The word in clinical or school-facing material without checking first.

How it works

One script, six rungs, pimped the way attendings pimp.

The pimp ladder

Each session takes one illness script and climbs it: recall, the next most common, telling it apart, workup, management, then a pearl. Every rung is harder than the last.

The session ends on your first miss. The rung you reach is your score, and your best rung on each script is kept.

Three attendings, the same scripts and the same grading. Only the pace and the tone change.

Pimps gently. Gives hints and waits for you.

PimpMe for Anki

Miss it on rounds, see it in Anki.

A small add-on for Anki on your computer. When you miss a question in PimpMe, it unsuspends the AnKing cards linked to that part of the script, so they turn up in your next review.

Download the add-on

For Anki on Windows, macOS and Linux. Works with the AnKing deck from AnkiHub.

What it does

  • Unsuspends the cards linked to what you missed
  • Tags those notes #PimpMe::missed
  • Syncs when Anki opens and closes
  • Puts each sync in one step you can undo
  • Sends review numbers for linked cards: ids, due date, interval, lapses

What it never does

  • Read or upload card text, card fronts or note fields
  • Touch cards you haven't missed on
  • Ask for a password in Anki
  • Run in AnkiMobile on the iPhone

Set it up once

  1. 1

    Download

    Get pimpme.ankiaddon with the button above.

  2. 2

    Install by double-clicking

    Anki opens and asks to install it. Restart Anki when it's done.

  3. 3

    Get a code

    In PimpMe, open Settings → Anki and tap Connect Anki. You'll see a code that works once, for 10 minutes.

  4. 4

    Enter it in Anki

    In Anki, choose Tools → PimpMe → Connect to PimpMe… and type the code. That's it.

What "Unsuspend 3 Anki cards" means

Each script is linked to AnKing notes by id, rung by rung. When a session ends on a miss, the result offers the cards linked to that rung. Tap it, and the next time Anki opens they're unsuspended and tagged, ready in your reviews.

Changed your mind? In Anki, Edit → Undo puts the whole sync back in one step.

Why is it called PimpMe?

Because that's what it's called on the wards. "Pimping" is medicine's word for an attending firing hard questions at a student, and it's been around since at least the 1980s. PimpMe takes your side: you ask for it, you practise in private, and you walk onto rounds ready.
